# Character restyle — cute round-faced agents with depth & distinction

**Date:** 2026-06-06
**Status:** Approved (design) — pending user review of this written spec
**Topic:** Restyle all six office agents into a cute, round-faced, big-eyed look; give
each a distinct identity (hair, outfit, face, skin tone); add directional depth shading,
a multi-tone skin ramp, and a floor contact shadow.

> Supersedes the original "minimal procedural depth-only" draft of this file. Depth
> shading is retained as one component (§5) inside a larger character restyle.
> Arm animation (walk-swing / typing) was prototyped but **dropped** at the user's
> request — it read as unnatural at the chibi proportions (see §9).

---

## 1. Problem & goal

Today's sprites ([js/app.js](../../../js/app.js), `drawSprite` + the `*_BD`/`*_BU` grids)
are flat, square-headed, small-eyed, and nearly identical between agents (Manager,
Reader, Writer share one face; only colors differ). They also float (no ground shadow).

Goal — make the agents:
1. **Cute** — round head, large sparkly eyes (reference-matched).
2. **Distinct** — each agent recognizable by hairstyle, outfit, face features, and skin tone.
3. **Dimensional** — directional light, a 4-tone skin ramp, and a floor contact shadow.

Non-goals: changing the roster/agents, room layout, data flow, hooks, polling, or the
existing leg walk-cycle. No build step at runtime, no framework, no new jQuery usage
(CLAUDE.md §1, §5). Cute "big head / small body" proportions are **kept** (user decision).

## 2. Validated previews

The finished look was rendered and approved via the §7 PNG workflow (`__cute_preview.png`:
the 6 characters with face, hair, outfit, skin ramp, depth, and contact shadow). The
throwaway `__*` scripts/PNGs are deleted before commit (CLAUDE.md §8/§10); the
validated **grids are generated by a Python helper and baked into `js/app.js`** (§4).

## 4. Sprite geometry & generation

- **Grid:** 36 cells wide. Head block = 26 rows (round dome hair + face ellipse), body
  block = 13 rows (torso + arms-at-rest + legs). Same 36-wide convention as today, so the
  existing `drawSprite` blit + `spriteScale` continue to work unchanged.
- **Round head:** the face is an ellipse of skin; hair is built per style around it
  (a top dome cap plus side-framing whose depth — `short`…`long` — produces the distinct
  silhouettes). Big eyes are stamped as dark ellipses (`e`) with a white highlight cluster
  (`i`); features (glasses/mustache/goatee/freckles/headphones/beret/crown) are stamped on top.
- **Generation (committed tool — decided):** a dev-only Python generator `py/gen_sprites.py`,
  **not run at runtime** (preserves the no-build-step rule), emits the validated grid
  arrays, which are pasted into `js/app.js`. It is kept in the repo because the grids are
  too complex to hand-edit later; rerun it to tweak a sprite, then re-paste the output.
  Every generated grid is asserted to be exactly 36 wide and to contain only allowed
  characters before emission — removing the silent page-blanking width/typo risk
  CLAUDE.md §5 warns of.

## 5. Color map & shading (in `drawSprite`)

`drawSprite` keeps its signature but gains a **`skinColor`** parameter (threaded like
`hairColor`, sourced per-agent) and these color-map keys:

- Skin ramp (derived from `skinColor`): `L` highlight ×1.12, `s` base, `d` mid-shadow
  ×0.84, `D` deep-shadow ×0.70.
- Hair: `h`, `H` (shadow ×0.72). Body: `b`, `B` (×1.28), `k` (×0.66).
- Accessories: `G` glasses/headphone metal, `i` eye/teeth white, `e` eye/facial-hair
  dark, `m` mouth, `c` crown/gold, `z` shoe; `p`/`P` headphone gray; `q`/`Q` charcoal
  beret; `r`/`R` maroon scarf.

Two shading layers compose:

1. **Baked skin ramp** — the generator places `L/d/D` cells (forehead/nose highlight;
   cheek & jaw mid-shadow; deep contact shadow directly under the hair fringe and hat
   brim). This is the sculpted "skin shader."
2. **Procedural directional pass** (computed at draw time, memoized per grid+colors): a
   top-left→bottom-right gradient (`hi 1.18`→`lo 0.66`, **Medium**) over form materials,
   an edge **rim** (away-edges ×0.80, lit-edges ×1.10), a **hair specular** (top-left
   hair blended toward white ~0.55), and a 1-px dark **outline** (`#0a0a16`) on the
   silhouette. Eyes/glasses/mouth are excluded so they stay crisp.

Memoization keeps per-frame cost equal to today's blit; the neighbor scans run only on cache miss.

## 6. Contact shadow (in `drawAgent`)

Before drawing the sprite, draw a soft ellipse at the feet (`agent.pos.x`, `agent.pos.y`):
radius ≈ `sprW·0.30` × `sprW·0.10`, soft radial gradient `rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`→transparent.
**Skipped when seated** (feet occluded by desk/chair). Pure canvas; no geometry/`NAV_OBSTACLES` change.

## 7. Frames & back (up-facing) sprites

- **Animation:** unchanged from today — the existing leg walk-cycle (`stand/walk1/walk2`)
  only. No arm swing, no typing frames (dropped, §9). Idle holds `stand`; walking cycles
  `stand, walk1, stand, walk2`; seated holds `stand`.
- **Back view:** every agent keeps its up/back view (used walking up and seated at the
  desk): the same hair silhouette from behind (no face), back of the outfit, plus the
  matching `stand/walk1/walk2` leg frames. Generated by the same helper.

## 8. Data flow

Unchanged — purely a rendering change. No `agent-events.json`, polling, or hook change.

## 9. Risks & decisions

- **Page-blanking width/typo errors** (CLAUDE.md §5) → generator asserts width and
  allowed-chars before emit; no hand-typed grids.
- **New `const`/key collisions** → unique names; verify the page renders after baking.
- **Performance** → memoized depth pass; per-frame blit unchanged.
- **Arm animation dropped** → prototyped (`__anim_preview`) but removed at user request:
  at "big head / small body" chibi proportions the swinging/typing arms looked unnatural
  and not cute. Walking is carried by the legs alone, as today.
